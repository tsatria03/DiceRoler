Welcome to my custom Dice Roller.
Custom Dice Roller is a flexible and accessible dice rolling application. It allows you to simulate rolling any number of dice with a wide range of die types.
You can add custom modifiers to your roll. The app plays satisfying audio feedback for each roll.

Features
You can roll up to 20 dice at once by selecting from a preset list. The app supports a wide variety of dice types, including D100, D30, D20, D16, D12, D8, D6, D4, D3, and D2.
You can apply custom modifiers ranging from -10 to +10 to your total. Realistic sound effects are played when rolling and landing dice.
The interface is fully keyboard-friendly and designed for screen reader users. After rolling, the results are spoken aloud with a detailed breakdown and total score.

How to Use
Launch the app to open the main Dice Roller interface. Select a dice type from the list, such as D20 or D6.
Choose how many dice you want to roll, from 1 to 20. Pick a custom modifier if you'd like to add or subtract from your roll.
Press the "Roll Dice" button to hear the dice shake and land, followed by your results.

Audio Cues
The file "shake.ogg" is played while the dice are rolling. The files "land1.ogg" and "land2.ogg" are used when the dice land and your result is spoken.

Enjoy, and happy rolling!
